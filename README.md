# String Tuner: Violin & Cello

String Tuner: Violin & Cello is a free offline android tuner for 60 instruments, with drone, metronome and intonation training. microphone audio is analysed live and never recorded. It is published by lapnito.cz s.r.o. and was last verified from the public store listing on 2026-10-01.

**Tune viola, bass & orchestra · Drone · Tempo · Intonation trainer**

- [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tomas.string_tuner)
- GitHub: https://github.com/Lapnito/string-tuner-violin-cello
- Support: https://github.com/Lapnito/string-tuner-violin-cello/issues

## Privacy and limits

The listing says microphone audio is analysed live and never recorded; no account, analytics or servers are used. Google Play declares no data collected or shared. A phone tuner is a practice aid. Room noise, microphone quality and playing technique can affect detection.
