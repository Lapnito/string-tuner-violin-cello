# String Tuner: Violin & Cello

A free offline Android tuner for 60 instruments, with drone, metronome and intonation training. Microphone audio is analysed live and never recorded.

- Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tomas.string_tuner
- Package: `com.tomas.string_tuner`
- Developer: lapnito.cz s.r.o.

A phone tuner is a practice aid. Room noise, microphone quality and playing technique can affect detection.
