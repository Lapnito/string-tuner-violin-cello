# Privacy in String Tuner: Violin & Cello

String Tuner: Violin & Cello is a free offline android tuner for 60 instruments, with drone, metronome and intonation training. microphone audio is analysed live and never recorded. It is published by lapnito.cz s.r.o. and was last verified from the public store listing on 2026-10-01.

## What the listing states

The listing says microphone audio is analysed live and never recorded; no account, analytics or servers are used. Google Play declares no data collected or shared.

## What this does not prove

Store declarations describe the published build and can change. Check the current data-safety panel before installation.
